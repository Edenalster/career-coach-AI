Junior AI Engineer

About the role
We are a small product team building AI-powered features for business customers. We are looking for a Junior AI Engineer to help us design, build and evaluate LLM-based applications, such as document question answering and workflow automation.

Responsibilities
- Build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines using vector databases and LLM APIs.
- Develop backend services and REST APIs that expose AI features to our web application.
- Evaluate the quality of AI outputs and improve prompts and retrieval strategies.
- Work with the product team to turn customer needs into working features.
- Package and deploy services using Docker.

Requirements
- B.Sc. in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
- Experience with Python.
- Hands-on experience with LLM APIs (such as OpenAI, Gemini or Anthropic).
- Familiarity with RAG concepts and vector databases.
- Experience building REST APIs.
- Experience with Git.
- Good English communication skills.

Nice to have
- Experience with LangChain or LangGraph.
- Docker and CI/CD.
- Experience with TypeScript or React.
- Experience evaluating LLM applications.
